A second doll, `ual`, in `skelly.body`: a figure built on the skeleton of Quaternius's Universal Animation Library mannequin, so a game can play UAL's clips on it. Its parts, `ual_bust`, `ual_arm` and `ual_leg`, are named after the bones they move with and stand on those bones' joints. Its rig, `dolls/ual.rig.json`, uses UAL's bone names and rest rotations. The manifest's `dolls` lists it. No garment is fitted to it yet.
