For game developers: the keyword `holdall` is gone, as the gym bags already carry `duffel`. Undead mobs carry the new
keyword `undead` beside `zombie`: every zombie, the acid zombies and the death-knight bestiary mob. The `place` facet
gains `bathroom`, `farm`, `hospital`, `kitchen`, `laboratory`, `office` and `workshop`, applied to the household
objects, tools, machines, appliances and instruments that clearly belong in them.
