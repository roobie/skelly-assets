Voxel firearms are now filled from the solids as authored rather than from their bevelled display meshes, which punched see-through pinholes where solids meet (the stock of the anti-materiel rifle, the receiver of the pipe rifle) and shaved the corners of boxes. Forty-one firearm models change, their sidecar datums shifting by under a millimetre; no other model does, and the smooth copies keep their bevel.
