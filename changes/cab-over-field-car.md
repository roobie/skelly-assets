For game developers: a new vehicle, `cab_over_field_car`, a hard-top forward-control light 4x4 in olive paint, about
1.7 t empty and 4 m long. The cab stands over the front axle on a raised floor, with the petrol four-cylinder behind
that axle between the front seats under a cover. It has two cab doors and a pair of rear doors, and reuses existing
engine, gearbox, seat and lamp parts.
