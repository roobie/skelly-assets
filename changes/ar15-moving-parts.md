The AR-15s now have moving parts. Each one has a bolt carrier and charging handle in `gun.action`, which draw back together through the 95.25 mm stroke at 700 rounds a minute. A gun's `gun.moving` lists the parts that move outside its action, each with its node and motion. On the AR-15s these are the ejection port cover, which swings open, and the carbines' collapsible stock, which slides through stops `extended`, `position-5` to `position-2` and `collapsed`.
