The AR-15s now have moving parts. Each one has a bolt carrier and charging handle in `gun.action`, which draw back together through the 95.25 mm stroke at 700 rounds a minute. The charging handle rides back clear over the stock. A gun's `gun.moving` lists the parts that move outside its action, each with its node and motion. On the AR-15s these are the ejection port cover and, on the carbines, the collapsible stock. The port cover turns out and down about 168° until it rests on the magazine well, and the carrier shows through the open port. The stock slides through stops `extended`, `position-5` to `position-2` and `collapsed`. The ejection port is now cut to the size of its cover and ends on the bolt face. The upper receiver is now 1.6 mm taller, so its roof over the charging handle is one voxel thick. The flat-top and handguard rails sit 1.6 mm higher with it, while the sight line stays where it was.
