`rifle_anti_materiel` now ships with iron sights: a fixed post on its barrel shroud and a removable one-piece peep
rear sight, attachment `rail-peep-rear-sight`, on its receiver rail. Both sights keep a smooth mesh in the voxel
gun, so the round hole and the post read cleanly at the eye. A scope fits that rail once the rear sight comes off.
