STANAG magazines and the AR-15 magazine wells they seat in now declare the `ar15-magazine` port standard in their port `extras`, so a magazine cut for another well, such as the SR-25's, no longer matches an AR-15 well. This changes `magazine_stanag_20`, `magazine_stanag_30`, `lower_ar`, `rifle_assault` and the AR-15 designs; their geometry is unchanged.
