For game developers: small-arms rounds and spent cases are drawn as 12-sided faceted lathes instead of voxels, so a
9 mm round keeps its rim, taper and bullet nose instead of breaking into uneven blocks. Each is a few hundred
triangles. A cartridge large enough for its grain, such as a tank-gun shell, is still voxelized, and a cartridge's data
may choose either look with `voxelize`.
