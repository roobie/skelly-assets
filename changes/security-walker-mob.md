For game developers: the boss mob `security-walker` is now exported in `skelly.bestiary`, as a GLB with its body and clips files like every other mob. It bakes `standing.idle`, `standing.walk` (its stomp, which states its stride in metres) and `standing.attack` (its twin-pod fire, which states its hit time). It has no far-LOD and no limping, dragging or crawling sets, and its body and clips use the same rig and clip format as the leaper's. The vehicle `security_walker` is a separate model.
