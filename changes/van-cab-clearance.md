The cab doors, bonnet and rear doors of the vans built on the shared van body no longer touch the wing, quarter glass,
dashboard, A pillars, slam panel, headlamps or rear posts when shut or swinging: each stands a millimetre clear. The
bonnet's front edge is a little thinner over the headlamps, and the tail lamps stand outboard of the rear doors' hinge
pins.
