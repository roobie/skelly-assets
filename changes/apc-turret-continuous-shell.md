The APC turret now has continuously sloping armour plates in its smooth copy instead of seven broad staircase bands. Its roof sits between the walls without overlapping them, and the gun mask has a closed cheek where it meets the shell. The voxel copy is sampled from the same continuous solids; fittings, gun ports, hatch motion, mass and placements are preserved.
Its collision boxes change on purpose: each sloped plate is stacked in short courses so the boxes follow the slope, and the core's 109 boxes become 122, covering 19.3% of the open interior rather than 15.8% (the fused turret: 142 become 155, 21.5% becomes 24.9%).
