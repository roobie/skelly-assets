Each bone of a doll's rig says how it is worn, in `bind.wears`: `part`, the name a worn model's `wornOn.part` gives, so a piece is seated on the UAL doll's bones by the same manifest entry as on the player's; `rest`, where a piece drawn on the bone sits in its node, read as `bind.rest` is; and `hides`, what each name of the piece's `covers` hides of the bone's own part on that doll.
