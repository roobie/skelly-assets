The 40 mm and 120 mm rounds (`round_40x365r`, `round_120x570` and their cases) look like shells: a nose ogive with a
fuze flat, a copper driving band on a new `band` material slot, and a primer sized to the case head. Every cartridge's
primer now shows a cup inside an anvil ring, at 32 more triangles a model; the 12-gauge shell, which had no primer,
gains one at 112.
