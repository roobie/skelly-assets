For game developers: the saloon and its derived cars, the estate, coupe, police car and semi tractor fit each wheel as a rim at its wheel site and a tyre at `<wheel>_tyre`, with a brake disc and caliper at `<wheel>_brake_disc` and `<wheel>_caliper`; turn only the wheel site.
These cars weigh 32 kg more for their brakes; `wheel_205_55r16` and `wheel_295_80r22_5` keep their ids and masses with spoked rims, and new lootable rows are `tyre_*`, `rim_*`, `brake_disc_*` and `brake_caliper_*`.
