Sci-fi wheeled carrier: `apc_scifi_wheeled`, a low, slab-sided armoured hull stepped down from its troop casemate to a
front deck, slung between four large 18.00 R25 flotation wheels, its nose a short faceted ogive like a bullet's. It
carries a driver's hood with a narrow visor slit, a boxy turret with a single cannon and a launcher pod on each cheek,
roof grab rails, stowage panniers and a rear ramp, all in flat dark earth paint. Its hull, flanks, axles, hood, visor,
weapon station, cannon, launchers, rails, panniers and ramp are separable rows of their own, tagged `sci-fi` and
`armoured`, in the ground-vehicles pack.

The kit gains the 18.00 R25 wheel `wheel_18_00r25` (tyre `tyre_18_00r25` on rim `rim_25_steel_wide`) and the darker
flat dark earth vehicle paint `paint-fde`. Compound prisms can be cut by sloped planes, so armour plates need not lie
along an axis.
