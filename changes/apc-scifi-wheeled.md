Sci-fi wheeled carrier: `apc_scifi_wheeled`, a low, blocky armoured hull of flat, chamfered plates slung between four
large 18.00 R25 flotation wheels, its nose drawn to a faceted point in plan and profile. It carries a driver's hood
with a narrow visor slit, a faceted turret with a single cannon and a launcher pod on each cheek, roof grab rails,
stowage panniers and a rear ramp, all in flat dark earth paint. Its hull, flanks, axles, hood, visor, weapon station,
cannon, launchers, rails, panniers and ramp are separable rows of their own, tagged `sci-fi` and `armoured`, in the
ground-vehicles pack.

The kit gains the 18.00 R25 wheel `wheel_18_00r25` (tyre `tyre_18_00r25` on rim `rim_25_steel_wide`) and the vehicle
paint `paint-fde`, the colour of the FDE polymer. Compound prisms can be cut by sloped planes, so armour plates need not
lie along an axis.
