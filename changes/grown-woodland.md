Grow the woodland's trees and shrubs from species instead of drawing them piece by piece. `woodland_oak_spreading`, `woodland_oak_upright`, `woodland_birch`, `woodland_hazel` and `woodland_shrub_low` keep their ids with grown shapes and new sizes (the oaks grow broader and taller); `woodland_scots_pine` and `woodland_spruce` are added beside the drawn `woodland_pine`, which stays. Every grown model also ships a `_sparse` twin, thinner in branching and foliage on the same trunk and limbs, for open woodland and scenes of many trees, and a `_sparser` twin on trial. The grown trees voxelize at the standard 0.5 m grain, so a tree's voxel file stays within about 2k triangles; the shrubs keep a finer grain. Grown wood carries the `bark` (or `bark-birch`) material id and is drawn plain until a bark shader draws its structure.
