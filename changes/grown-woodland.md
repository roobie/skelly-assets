Grow the woodland's trees and shrubs from species instead of drawing them piece by piece. `woodland_oak_spreading`, `woodland_oak_upright`, `woodland_birch`, `woodland_hazel` and `woodland_shrub_low` keep their ids with grown shapes and new sizes (the oaks grow broader and taller); `woodland_scots_pine` and `woodland_spruce` are added beside the drawn `woodland_pine`, which stays. Every grown model also ships a `_sparse` twin, thinner in branching and foliage on the same trunk and limbs, for open woodland and scenes of many trees. A grown tree's voxel file stays within about 22k triangles.
