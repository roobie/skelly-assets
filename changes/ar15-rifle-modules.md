The gun authoring tool builds two more modular AR-15 designs, `ar15-rifle-a2` (20 inch A2 rifle with carry handle and fixed stock) and `ar15-carbine-front-sight` (16 inch carbine with front sight base and rail rear sight), from the same lower and parts as the free-float carbine. Their front sight base comes as the forged A2 triangle, whose front is one face from its front strut down past the barrel and whose sling loop sits centred under that longer ring, or as a straight sight block. The free-float carbine's rail now stands at a measured flat-top's height, 3.8 mm lower, with its handguard rail flush with the receiver's, and the rail rear sight keeps its aperture on the flat-top sight plane. No pack exports these AR-15 designs or their parts yet, so no pack id changes; they reach the packs when `rifle_assault` is built from them. The smooth pack's `revolver`, `revolver_cylinder`, `shotgun_pump` and `tube_magazine` files change in the same shape, since every gun's octagons now come from one builder: the pump's chamfered bands list their corners from another start, and the revolver's octagons differ in the last bit.
